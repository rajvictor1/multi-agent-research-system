TOPIC: EV battery market size, and where analyst estimates disagree
TIMEFRAME: 2024-2026
SUBTOPICS:
- global market size estimates by year
- regional demand split (China, Europe, North America, India)
- cell and pack price trends per kWh
- raw material and supply chain constraints
- policy and subsidy changes affecting demand
- points where published estimates conflict, and why

USE CASE: an EV startup sizing the market for an investor deck. The number that goes in
the pitch has to be defensible, which means knowing the consensus range AND the outliers.
Averaging a $91B estimate with a $12B one produces a figure no analyst will back.
