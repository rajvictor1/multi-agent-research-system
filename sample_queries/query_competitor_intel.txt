TOPIC: Competitor feature launches and pricing in project-management SaaS
TIMEFRAME: 2025-2026
SUBTOPICS:
- features shipped in the last four quarters
- announced roadmaps and launch dates
- list pricing by tier
- what customer reviews say about value for money
- enterprise contract and support differences
- claims that vendor marketing and third-party reviews disagree on

USE CASE: a product manager planning a roadmap. Vendor pages and review sites disagree on
purpose, so the report must show both rather than pick the flattering one.
