TOPIC: Clinical evidence for continuous glucose monitoring in type 2 diabetes
TIMEFRAME: 2023-2026
SUBTOPICS:
- randomized trial results and effect sizes
- sample sizes and study quality
- regulatory filings and approvals
- reported adverse events
- cost effectiveness studies
- studies that contradict the main findings

USE CASE: investor due diligence. A study with n=500 and one with n=50 are not equal
evidence, and a report that averages their results hides exactly the thing the decision
turns on. Confidence must track study quality, and both results must appear.
