TOPIC: Transformer models for time-series forecasting -- what is settled and what is not
TIMEFRAME: 2023-2026
SUBTOPICS:
- reported accuracy gains over classical baselines
- which datasets and benchmarks were used
- criticisms and failed replications
- computational cost compared to simpler models
- performance on non-stationary data
- open questions the papers themselves name

USE CASE: a literature review. "Paper A reports 15%, Paper B reports 5% on a different
dataset" is the actual state of the field. Collapsing that into "roughly 10%" is the
overclaim a reviewer will catch.
