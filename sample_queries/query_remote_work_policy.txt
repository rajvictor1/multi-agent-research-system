TOPIC: Impact of remote work on urban commercial real estate prices
TIMEFRAME: 2023-2026
SUBTOPICS:
- national average price and vacancy changes
- city-level outliers such as San Francisco and New York
- office-to-residential conversion activity
- municipal tax revenue effects
- how far the published data actually runs
- studies whose conclusions conflict, and their methodology differences

USE CASE: policy research. A -20% figure from one city and a -5% national average are
both true and mean different things. The report has to say which is which, and say plainly
where the data stops.
