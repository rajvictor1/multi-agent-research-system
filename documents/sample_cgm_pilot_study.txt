SOURCE: Regional Diabetes Care Network (fictional) -- pilot study, preprint
TITLE: A single-centre pilot of continuous glucose monitoring in community care
PUBLISHED: 2024-06-30
DESIGN: single centre, non-randomized, 6 months, preprint -- NOT peer reviewed

--- Abstract, p.1 ---

n = 48 participants (all CGM, no control arm).

RESULT: HbA1c fell 0.5 percentage points at 6 months.
Responder rate (HbA1c below 7.0%): 41%.

--- Section 3  Discussion, p.5 ---

Our responder rate is well below figures reported by hospital-based trials. We see
three likely reasons:

1. Our population is community primary care, older, and has longer diabetes
   duration than typical trial cohorts.
2. Six months of follow-up, not twelve.
3. 11 of 48 participants stopped wearing the sensor before month 4, and were
   counted as non-responders.

--- Section 4  Limitations, p.6 ---

Small sample, no control arm, no randomization, not peer reviewed. These results
should be treated as hypothesis-generating and should not be pooled with
randomized trial data.
