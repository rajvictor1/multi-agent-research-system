SOURCE: Journal of Metabolic Medicine (fictional) -- pivotal trial report
TITLE: Continuous glucose monitoring in adults with type 2 diabetes:
       a randomized, multi-centre trial
PUBLISHED: 2025-11-04
DESIGN: randomized, controlled, open-label, 12 months, 14 sites

--- Abstract, p.1 ---

n = 512 participants (256 CGM, 256 standard fingerstick care).
Primary endpoint: change in HbA1c at 12 months.

RESULT: HbA1c fell 1.1 percentage points in the CGM arm versus 0.4 points in the
control arm. Between-group difference 0.7 points (95% CI 0.5-0.9, p<0.001).

Responder rate (HbA1c below 7.0% at 12 months): 78% CGM vs 46% control.

--- Section 4.2  Adverse events, p.9 ---

Skin irritation at the sensor site: 14% of CGM participants, all mild.
Two serious adverse events, both adjudicated as unrelated to the device.
No episodes of severe hypoglycaemia in either arm.

--- Section 5.1  Cost, p.12 ---

Incremental cost per quality-adjusted life year: $28,400 over a 10-year horizon.
Sensitivity analysis: remains under $50,000/QALY unless sensor price rises above
$92/month.

--- Section 6  Limitations, p.14 ---

Open-label design; participants knew their arm. Sites were urban tertiary centres,
so results may not transfer to primary care. Follow-up ends at 12 months, so
durability beyond one year is unknown.
